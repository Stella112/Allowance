# Allowance: 3-minute demo video script

How to record: open `https://allowance-two.vercel.app/pitch`, press **Play**, and screen-record (Windows: **Win+Alt+R**). Read each line when its scene appears. The scenes advance on their own, timed to this script. Speak warmly and a little slower than normal. About 140 words a minute.

---

## 0:00–0:30 · The hook (the most important 30 seconds)

**Scene 1: the night sky, a kid asleep, a plane crossing.**

> It's 2am in Lagos. Tobi is asleep.
> In London, his aunty just finished work, and she's sending him money, like millions of families do every night.
> By breakfast, that money will be spent.
>
> What if, instead, Tobi woke up owning a piece of Apple?

**Scene 2: the Allowance logo.**

> This is Allowance: the family money app that works the night shift.

## 0:30–0:55 · The problem

**Scene 3: "Money sent home gets spent."**

> Kids in America grow up with apps like Greenlight that teach them to invest in real stocks.
> Kids in Nigeria, and most of the world, don't, even though real US stocks now live on Base.
> And families are split across time zones. Gifts arrive overnight as cash, and cash disappears.

## 0:55–1:55 · The solution: one night shift

**Scene 4: midnight. Coins drop into the savings jar.**

> Allowance does its work while kids sleep.
> At midnight, their weekly allowance lands in their savings, automatically, on Base.

**Scene 5: the bedtime pitch with the coach.**

> At bedtime, kids pitch a company they like. Apple, because everyone wants AirPods.
> Before it reaches Mum, an AI coach asks one question: "What could go wrong?" Every investment becomes a lesson.

**Scene 6: London and Lagos clocks. The gift flies in.**

> Across the world, Aunty opens a gift link. She sees it's 9pm in Lagos and picks a slice of Apple. Tobi will wake up to it.

**Scene 7: breakfast. The Face ID approval.**

> In the morning, Mum approves the pitch with Face ID, matches 50%, and real shares are bought.

**Scene 8: the Morning Report.**

> And Tobi wakes up to his Morning Report: his allowance, his gift, his approved pitch, each one a real transaction on Base.
> He records a thank-you that's waiting for Aunty when she wakes up.

## 1:55–2:30 · Why Allowance

**Scene 9: what makes it different.**

> Allowance is real: real USDC, real Coinbase tokenized stocks, bought through Aerodrome on Base mainnet.
> It's safe. Kids can only ask, never move money. Every approval needs Face ID. Spending caps live in the smart contract itself.
> And it's built for families across time zones: overnight gifts, group gifts from cousins, and graduation at 18, when every share moves to their own wallet.

## 2:30–3:00 · The close

**Scene 10: "Try it in 2 minutes."**

> You can live a full night shift right now, with no wallet and no sign-up. Every step is a real transaction.
>
> Allowance. While they sleep, they grow.
> A child who grows up owning companies, and takes them into adulthood.

---

Tips:
- Smile while you record the first and last lines. It changes your voice.
- If you use a natural voice tool (for example ElevenLabs), paste each section, export the audio, and line it up with the screen recording in Clipchamp, which comes free with Windows.
- Keep the final video under 3:00.
